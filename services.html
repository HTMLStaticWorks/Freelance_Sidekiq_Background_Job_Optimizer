Infrastructure Optimization Services

We specialize in the deep technical layers of Ruby on Rails background processing. From Redis tuning to Ruby memory management, we make your jobs faster and more reliable.

Queue Performance Tuning

Generic Sidekiq setups often suffer from "noisy neighbor" problems. We implement advanced queue partitioning, weighted prioritization, and Redis shard balancing to ensure your critical jobs never wait.

  • Latency monitoring & reduction
  • Redis connection pool optimization
  • Queue sharding strategies

Worker Memory Optimization

Sidekiq processes are notorious for bloating over time. We profile your workers to identify memory leaks, optimize ObjectSpace usage, and tune Ruby GVL concurrency to maximize RAM efficiency.

  • RSS bloat identification
  • jemalloc integration
  • Concurrency vs RAM balancing

Dead Job Recovery & Retries

Don't let your dead queue become a black hole. We implement smart retry logic, exponential backoff tuning, and automated recovery workflows for transient failures.

  • Failure pattern analysis
  • Dead queue automation
  • Custom error grouping
